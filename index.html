Growth DE and geometry trajectory

dL/dt = η kL L   ·   dR/dt = η kR R

This browser version uses the exact solution of the same constant-rate ODE integrated by SciPy in the Python app: L = L₀ exp(η kL t), R = R₀ exp(η kR t). Equal transfer factors preserve the jointly calibrated length/radius trajectory.

Healthy-child cross-sectional means are a growth proxy, not observed longitudinal residual-limb growth. The baseline is synthetic, not a patient scan.

Numerical and interpolation sensitivity

Different meshes and contact penalties change absolute pressures and conditional dates. Full convergence and clinical validity are not established. The 1° lifting animation uses a different loading path.

TRANSPARENT ASSUMPTIONS

Evidence & limits

What is computed

17 verified FEBio cases: 15 calibration nodes across three numerical profiles, one enlargement case and one independent interpolation holdout. Three cases reuse verified earlier results; 14 are newly solved.

Growth forecast: 100 mm limb, 20/15 mm proximal/distal radii, 50 g object, 200 mm lever. Joint radial growth: 0–0.5%, within the age-9-to-10 trajectory.

What the date means

The first modeled crossing of an assumed relative P95 increase. This is a reassessment trigger, not a required replacement date or a clinical safety boundary.

Predictions stop at +0.5% radial growth or age 10. Unsupported combinations produce no date. Slow/reference/fast are assumed growth scenarios, not confidence bounds.

Validation

The independent +0.22% growth holdout has a P95 interpolation error of approximately 0.0834%. This checks local interpolation; it does not establish global accuracy, mesh convergence or clinical validity.
